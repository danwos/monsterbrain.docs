# Backlog

`Ctrl 3` lists the folder's backlog: the larger tasks for later and the open
questions that are not part of any work yet. Most of them become a concept one
day; the steps of a running concept stay in that concept. The **Backlog**
button counts what is still pending – open items and those already taken up
by a concept.

```{figure} ../_static/screens/backlog.webp
:alt: The backlog view: open items grouped by project, one item in a concept, an open question opened in the middle

An open question of the tea shop; below it the task a concept already took up.
```

## What you see

| Group | |
|---|---|
| Open | the items nobody works on yet, under one header per project |
| In a concept | the items a concept took up, the project as a grey hint |
| Closed | done and discarded items, also those in the archive – folded, a click on the header opens it |

Each row shows a mark for what the item is – a square for a task, a round `?`
for a question –, its title, its status when it is not open, and whether it
changed. Hovering shows the summary, kind, creation date, where it came from,
the concept started from it and the path. When the folder holds several
areas, each area gets its own groups.

A click opens the item in the preview tab, a double or `Ctrl` click in a tab
of its own. `j` and `k` step through the pending items in the order of the
view.

Where items live and what their frontmatter holds: [Your folder](folders.md#conventions-it-understands).
