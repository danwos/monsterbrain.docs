# deciwa

[deciwa](https://deciwa.danwos.dev) is the danwos review tool. As a
monsterbrain extension it adds:

- **review on the rendered file** – Markdown, HTML, code and the new side of
  a diff,
- **state in the tree and the change view** – what is open, approved, stale
  or frozen, and which questions wait for an answer,
- **the feedback of the active file** in the right panel,
- **questions instead of `[CHECK]` markers.**

Review state – seen, approved, done – lives in deciwa, not in monsterbrain
({need}`DEC_NO_INTERACTION_STATE`). Without the extension monsterbrain works
as before, just without review.

Setup and the review keys: [deciwa in monsterbrain](https://deciwa.danwos.dev/integrations/monsterbrain.html).
