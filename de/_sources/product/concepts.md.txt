# Concepts

`Ctrl 4` lists the folder's concepts by title – open ones above closed ones,
newest first. Each concept shows its folder and the files its frontmatter
names under `results:`, so you see at a glance what a piece of work produced,
also outside its own folder.

A concept with `monster:` in its frontmatter shows that monster title in
italics below its own title.

```{figure} ../_static/screens/concepts.webp
:alt: The concepts view: open and closed concepts, one opened with its results

An open concept with the files it produced; closed concepts below.
```
